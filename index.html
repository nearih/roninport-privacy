roninport Privacy Policy

Developer: ronindev
Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the roninport Android app ("the app") handles your information.

Your documents stay on your device

The app reads the PDF portfolio statements you choose to import and builds your investment report entirely on your phone. Your statements, holdings, and reports are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.

Price updates

When you tap refresh, the app requests current market prices for the assets in your portfolio from Yahoo Finance. This request contains only the asset ticker symbols. It does not include your name, account details, or the amounts you hold. As with any internet request, Yahoo may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Yahoo's handling of that information is covered by the Yahoo Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding ads, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ronindev.app@gmail.com.
